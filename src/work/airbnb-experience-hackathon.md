<p>Planning a trip often means piecing together flights, accommodations, and activities across different platforms. For a group project in <i>The Business Potential of Generative AI</i>, my team and I identified a customer experience gap between Airbnb {% ext "https://www.airbnb.com/", "‘Stays’" %} and {% ext "https://www.airbnb.com/experiences", "‘Experiences’" %}, its offering of activities hosted by locals. We imagined how those offerings could connect more naturally, making the transition from booking a stay to planning activities more intuitive.</p>

<p>Using Lovable and ChatGPT, we developed and tested five iterations of an AI-powered itinerary prototype. Early versions were functional but struggled to interpret trip details and offered generic recommendations. By refining prompts and integrating destination-specific information (weather data, flight info, etc.) we worked toward more useful, personalized itineraries.</p>

<p>Shortly after our course ended, {% ext "https://news.airbnb.com/airbnb-2025-summer-release", "Airbnb redesigned its app" %} to recommend experiences based on your stay and organize your bookings into a daily itinerary. Sound familiar? {% pending "The announcement made its way into our group chat" %} <i>pretty</i> quickly.</p>
