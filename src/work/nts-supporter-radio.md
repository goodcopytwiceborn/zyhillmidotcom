<p>Some of my favorite songs came to me through {% ext "https://www.nts.live/", "NTS community radio" %}. Songs that aren’t on Spotify, or any other streaming platform.</p>

<p>In second hand stores, I’ll sometimes come across records I wouldn’t usually reach for. I like that browsing gives me the chance to step outside my algorithm. Without anything specific in mind, I can pick up a record because I’m curious about it. Since high school, I’ve wanted to buy these records and give them a new life.</p>

<p>I’d love to put those finds together for an NTS show through its {% ext "https://www.nts.live/supporters", "supporter program" %}. The only thing I’m missing is a record player.</p>
