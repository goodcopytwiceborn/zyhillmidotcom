<p>Readers want more context before committing their attention. Big blocks of text can feel overwhelming, but looking through smaller chunks can lead to scrolling fatigue, too. At the same time, {% ext "https://www.digicatapult.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/20200715_DC_142_AOTFReport_Digital.pdf", "there seems to be an appetite for more participatory and multi-sensory ways of collecting knowledge and experiencing media" %}.</p>

<p>What could writing learn from these experiences? Looking to {% ext "https://w3.org/WAI/gettingstarted", "accessibility design" %}, I’d like to challenge the way we think about interacting with text.</p>
