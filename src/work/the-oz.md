<p>AI is often marketed as something magical, with little acknowledgment of the human labor that makes it possible. Thinking back to the history of automation, from the {% ext "https://www.eliwhitney.org/cotton-gin-and-its-legacies", "cotton gin" %} to {% ext "https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Mechanical_Turk", "Amazon Mechanical Turk" %}, technological progress hasn’t necessarily meant better working conditions.</p>

<p>I want to investigate the working lives of data center technicians, particularly those entering tech through {% ext "https://careers.microsoft.com/us/en/datacenteracademy", "career development programs" %} for people from low-income backgrounds, and whether these jobs deliver on the opportunities promised.</p>
