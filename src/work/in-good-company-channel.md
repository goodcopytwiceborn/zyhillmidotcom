<p><i>In Good Company</i> is a growing collection of resources for people working across the spectrum of art and computing. Conversations about these fields often center Los Angeles, San Francisco, and New York City, but talent and community exist wherever people are creating, organizing, and gathering.</p>

<p>Inspired by my own search for creative community while at home in Georgia and the resource-sharing spirit of Mindy Seu’s {% ext "https://www.are.na/block/37872034", "Job Opportunities" %} and <i>{% ext "https://www.wordsofmouth.org/", "Words of Mouth" %}</i>, this channel centralizes event info, with particular attention to the South, Midwest, and other often-overlooked regions.</p>
