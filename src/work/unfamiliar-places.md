<p>I’ve inherited plenty from my family. A sense of direction was apparently not among those things. However, I did inherit a caution toward unfamiliar places. Their experiences put boundaries on what felt accessible, and, in turn, what felt possible for me.</p>

<p>Seeing {% pending "this" %} project inspired me to unpack this generational fear and anxiety, by revisiting photos from my travels. This ‘distance’ between an unfamiliar place as we imagine it and as we experience it.</p>
