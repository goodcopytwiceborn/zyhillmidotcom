<p>Conversation holds my attention in a way that other formats don’t, especially when the topic is complex. My time at {% ext "https://storycorps.org/", "StoryCorps" %} and {% ext "https://reachingandrooted.org/about-reaching-and-rooted", "Reaching and Rooted" %} showed me the {% ext "http://oralhistory.columbia.edu/blog-posts/the-journey-of-a-story", "value of preserving these exchanges" %} and how much you can do with a transcript.</p>

<p>I’d like to find a medium to preserve dialogue from the events I attend, both to document what I hear and to motivate me to become a more regular attendee.</p>
