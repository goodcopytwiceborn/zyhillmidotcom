<p>Service design considers how people, processes, and interactions work together to create an experience. The Netflix Experience is a shared service philosophy for {% ext "https://jobs.netflix.com/careers/content", "Studio Operations &amp; Services" %}, the team supporting productions, talent, crew, and vendors. This project looked at how those principles are understood and put into practice across the organization.</p>

<p>Interviewees and survey respondents reflected on how tenure, role, and region inform their understanding of the principles and their approach to everyday work.</p>

<p>Following the study, we considered how enablement could (1) build on company culture, (2) give teams resources, and (3) make progress easier to measure. These considerations went on to inform the operational strategy I developed for rolling out a continuing education initiative.</p>
